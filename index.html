A few good questions.

What details do I need for a quote?

A box style, approximate quantity, and your contact details are a good start. Dimensions, printing requirements, and information about your product help us understand the project.

Can I request a custom size?

Yes—choose “Custom sizes” in the quote form and share the dimensions you have in mind. Our team will review your requirements and discuss suitable options.

What if I don't know which box I need?

Select “I need help choosing” and tell us what you're packing and how it will be shipped. You can leave the dimensions blank.

How do I discuss pricing and timing?

Pricing and lead times depend on your specifications, quantity, and project needs. Include your target date in the form, and we'll discuss these details with your quote.
